Physically retire Qwen 2.5 from the Mac: unload launchd services for 32B/72B, delete the
two model directories plus the leftover qwen72b.3bit experiment directory, and delete the
two retired plists. Capture before/after evidence (df, ls, launchctl) into

"physical world" half; 19-02 is the "source code world" half.
