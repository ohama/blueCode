## Analysis of Gate Failures

**W1 FAIL:** 32B used `edit_file` then `write_file` with thought `"The file has been edited to fix the bug. Now, I will save the corrected version."` Fix 3 hint (`edit_file persists the change. After a successful edit_file, do NOT call write_file with the same path.`) was not sufficient to override this reasoning pattern.

**W2 FAIL:** Fix 3 partially worked — `write_file` was eliminated. However, the model substituted a verification `read_file` at step 3 (`"I will verify the changes by reading the updated content"`), maintaining 4 total steps. Net: step count unchanged, different 4-step path.

**T1 CANARY-WARN:** Model attempted `run_shell {"command": "echo $((2**10))"}` (SecurityDenied), then `echo 2^10 | bc` (1024), then `final: 1024`. Baseline was direct 1-step answer. Likely model variance; all baseline phaseA T1 32B runs were 1 step. Not attributed to a specific system prompt change.

## Overall Phase 9.1 Verdict

**ESCALATE to Plan 09.1-04.**

Fix 1 (dispatcher partial-bounds) and Fix 2 (truncated system-prompt hint) are validated. The primary audit
failures (T6 32B LoopGuard, T6 72B MaxLoops) are fully resolved.
