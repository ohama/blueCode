## Milestone Summary

**Decimal Phases:** None.

**Key Decisions:**

- **TST-01 single combined commit** (Phase 12): 4 mechanically-coupled file edits committed atomically. CLAUDE.md commit protocol allows this — atomic per task, not per file. Single `refactor` commit cleaner than 4 broken-build intermediate states.
- **TST-01 `let` not `let private`** (Phase 12): Module-public visibility required for cross-module `open` exposure. ROADMAP SC2 grep regex (`let makeMockResponse` no `private`) also depends on this.
- **TST-01 scope discipline** (Phase 12): `toolCall`, `mockLlm`/`stubLlm`, `mockToolsOk`/`stubToolsOk`, `discardStep` deliberately NOT factored. 3 prior milestones deferred TST-01 partly due to scope creep temptation; v1.4 ships the small win cleanly.
- **BENCH-06 single-line trap, defense-in-depth preserved** (Phase 13): Existing in-line heredoc-restore blocks at lines 133-151 (gate W1+W2) and 274-292 (write_W1/W2) kept unchanged. Heredoc handles between-invocation reset; trap handles exit-time cleanup. Either alone is sufficient; together they guarantee a clean working tree under every reasonable failure mode.
